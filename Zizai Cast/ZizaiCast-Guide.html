Zizai Cast · User guide

Your phone and your PC,
connected your way.

Zizai Cast does two things: phone mirroring puts your phone’s screen on this PC; remote control works the other way round, so you can control this PC from your phone. Every part is written as “what you want to do → a few steps → what success looks like → what to do if it doesn’t work”. Jump straight in from the table below.

0Before you start

The phone finds the PC over the network. Most “I can’t find Zizai Cast” cases come down to one of these.

  1. Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi. A PC plugged into the same router with a cable is fine. Mobile data, a personal hotspot or a guest Wi-Fi do not count.
  2. Allow the firewall the first time. When Windows shows “Windows Security Alert”, tick Private networks and click Allow access. If you clicked Cancel, see here.
  3. Turn off VPNs. With a VPN on the PC or the phone, the phone often can’t see the PC.

What you see when you open Zizai Cast

The window has two tabs at the top: Phone mirroring and Remote control. The phone mirroring tab says Choose your phone with three cards: click the button on the card for your phone.

Closing the window doesn’t quit: it asks whether to Quit or Keep running. Keep running puts it next to the clock at the bottom right, and the window comes back as soon as a phone connects.

1iPhone mirroring

What you want to doShow your iPhone / iPad screen and sound on the PC. No app to install.

  1. Zizai Cast is running on the PC (its window or its icon at the bottom right).
  2. Open Control Center on the iPhone: swipe down from the top-right corner (Face ID models) or up from the bottom (models with a round Home button).
  3. Tap Screen Mirroring (two overlapping rectangles).
  4. Tap Zizai Cast in the list.
Success looks like thisWithin a few seconds the phone’s screen appears on the PC with “Connected: phone name … Toolbar at the top”. On the first connection the toolbar explains: “Your tools live in this bar: screenshot, record, translate, zoom. Move the mouse to the top of the picture to bring it back; it stays while the pointer is on it.” While the toolbar is hidden, a thin pink line at the top of the picture shows where it is.
If it doesn’t work
  • “Zizai Cast” isn’t in the list: see Can’t find Zizai Cast.
  • Picture but no sound: turn up the iPhone’s volume buttons; see Sound volume.
  • It asks for a 4-digit PIN: type the digits shown on the PC (only when Ask new phones for a PIN is on in Settings).

Stop mirroring

iPhone Control Center → Screen Mirroring → Stop Mirroring; or on the PC press CtrlD or the red Disconnect button at the right end of the toolbar. When the iPhone locks, the picture pauses and continues by itself after unlocking.

Four places to disconnect (iPhone and Android alike): the red button at the right of the toolbar, CtrlD, Disconnect at the top of the right-click menu, and right-click on the icon at the bottom right → Disconnect (phone name). A recording in progress is saved first.

2Android casting (Miracast)

What you want to doShow an Android phone on the PC, to watch only. Nothing to set up, but the PC needs a Wi-Fi adapter that supports “wireless display” (most laptops do). It needs a PC that supports wireless display; on a PC that does not, Zizai Cast shows no Miracast option at all: use “Android: control from the PC” instead.

  1. Zizai Cast is running and the PC’s Wi-Fi is on (even if the PC uses a network cable).
  2. On the phone, swipe down twice from the top and find the cast tile: Samsung calls it Smart View, Xiaomi Cast, OPPO Screencast, vivo Screen mirroring.
  3. Tap Zizai Cast in the list; the first time can take 10–20 seconds.
Success looks like thisThe phone’s screen appears on the PC with “Connected”. To stop: tap the same tile again on the phone, or press CtrlD on the PC.
If it doesn’t work
  • The “Android: just watch” card is grey with a “Why?” link: this PC can’t receive Miracast. Click the link, or use Android control instead.
  • Google Pixel has no Miracast (its “Cast” is for Chromecast only): use Android control.
  • Stuck on “Connecting”: cancel on the phone, wait 10 seconds and try again, or see Miracast doesn’t connect.

3Android control (QR pairing, USB)

What you want to doSee an Android phone on the PC and use it with the mouse and keyboard. About 5 minutes to set up the first time, automatic after that. Needs Android 11 or later.

Step 1: turn on “Developer options” (once)

  1. Phone: Settings → About phone (Samsung: then Software information).
  2. Find Build number and tap it 7 times; it may ask for the phone’s unlock code.
  3. When it says “You are now a developer”, you’re done. Settings now has Developer options (usually under “System” or at the bottom).

The pairing window on the PC has these steps too: ① Developer options not on yet? See the steps. Where “Build number” is: Samsung: About phone → Software information; Xiaomi / Redmi: My device → All specs → OS version (or MIUI version), tap that 7 times; Pixel: About phone, at the bottom. Developer options then is at the bottom of Settings (Samsung), under Additional settings (Xiaomi) or under System (Pixel).

Step 2: pair with a QR code (once)

  1. Phone: Developer options → turn on Wireless debugging; tap Allow when asked.
  2. PC: on the waiting screen’s “Android: control from the PC” card, click Show QR code (or right-click menu → Connect a phone → Connect Android (scan QR)).
  3. Phone: tap the words “Wireless debugging” to open it → Pair device with QR code, and point it at the QR code on the PC.
Success looks like thisThe pairing window closes by itself, the phone’s screen appears, and the PC says “Connected: phone name. Toolbar at the top; right-click = Back”. From then on it connects by itself whenever Wireless debugging is on and you’re on the same Wi-Fi.
If it doesn’t work
  • Stuck on “Waiting for the phone to scan…”: phone and PC aren’t on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks don’t work). You can also click Use pairing code, tap “Pair device with pairing code” on the phone and type the phone’s IP address:port and 6-digit code into the PC.
  • Pairing failed: the window shows a new QR code; tap “Pair device with QR code” on the phone again and rescan.
  • Nobody scanned for a while: the QR code dims with Timed out and the window says “Stopped waiting. Check the same Wi-Fi and that Wireless debugging is on…”. Click New QR code to get a new code and scan again.
  • It doesn’t reconnect after a restart: Android turns Wireless debugging off when the phone restarts, and Zizai Cast says once “Can’t find the phone you paired before. If it was just restarted, open Developer options and turn “Wireless debugging” back on, then try again”. Turn it back on in Developer options; no need to pair again.

No Wi-Fi? Use a USB cable

  1. Phone: Developer options → turn on USB debugging.
  2. Plug the phone into the PC. When it asks “Allow USB debugging?”, tick Always allow from this computer and tap Allow.
  3. PC: right-click menu → Connect a phone → Connect over USB cable.
Success looks like this“Connecting over USB…”, then the phone’s screen.
If it doesn’t work
  • “No Android phone found on USB”: try a cable that carries data (some only charge), check USB debugging is on, unplug and plug in again.
  • The phone never asks to allow: pull down the notifications, set the USB mode to “File transfer” and try again.

4Recording and screenshots

What you want to doTake a picture of the phone screen, or record it as a video (MP4 with sound). Works once the phone’s picture is shown.

  1. Move the mouse to the top of the phone picture; the toolbar appears.
  2. Screenshot: click Shot (or CtrlS).
  3. Recording: click Rec (or CtrlR); a red dot and the seconds appear at the top left. Click again to stop.
  4. Find the files: right-click menu → Open screenshots folder / Open recordings folder.
Success looks like this“Screenshot saved in Pictures\截圖” after a screenshot; click View at its end to open the picture (the message stays 8 seconds, and as long as the pointer is on it); “Recording saved: file name” when a recording stops. A small row of buttons Open folder · Send to phone appears at the bottom.
If it doesn’t work
  • The buttons are grey with “Once a phone is connected”: get the phone’s picture on screen first.
  • Recording refused: there is less than 300 MB free on the disk.
  • The phone disconnected while recording: what was recorded is saved automatically.

5Pen

What you want to doDraw lines, arrows or highlights on the mirrored picture to explain something. The drawing stays on the PC; nothing is drawn on the phone.

  1. Move the mouse to the top of the picture and click Pen on the toolbar (or CtrlE).
  2. Hold the left button and drag to draw. The toolbar now has Tool (pen → highlighter → arrow), Color, Thickness, Undo and Clear.
  3. Press Esc (or CtrlE again) to stop drawing; the drawing stays. CtrlShiftE clears everything.
Success looks like this“Pen mode: drag on the screen to draw (Esc to stop)”, a pink frame round the picture, and a small “Pen mode · Esc or Ctrl+E to stop” label at the bottom left. Screenshots and recordings include the drawing by default (right-click menu → Pen to change that).
If it doesn’t work
  • With Android control, drawing taps the phone: in pen mode the mouse stops controlling the phone; press Esc or CtrlE before using the phone again.
  • Wrong stroke: CtrlZ undoes the last one.

6Drop files and send to phone

What you want to doPut photos, videos or documents from the PC, or the screenshot you just took, on your phone.

Way 1: drag files onto the Zizai Cast window

  1. Select the files in File Explorer and drag them over the Zizai Cast window (don’t let go yet): the window says what will happen.
  2. Let go.
Success looks like thisWith Android control: the files go straight onto the phone (an .apk is installed), with “Sent to the phone: name” or “Installed: name”. iPhone, Miracast or no phone: a QR code appears on the PC; scan it as in step 3 of Way 2.

Way 2: Send to phone (QR code)

  1. Right-click menu → Send to phone → Send the last screenshot to the phone (or Send to phone… to pick files). The toolbar has this button too.
  2. With several new files you first tick which ones to send, then click Send N.
  3. With the phone on the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code on the PC with the camera and open the page. Photos: long-press → “Add to Photos” (iPhone) or “Download image” (Android); videos: tap “Download video”.
Success looks like thisThe phone opens a page listing the files, ready to save to the photo library.
If it doesn’t work
  • The page doesn’t open: the phone isn’t on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks and mobile data don’t work).
  • “Only files can be dropped here (not folders)”: drag the files inside the folder, or zip the folder first.
  • The link expired: links last 10 minutes; send again.

7Translate

What you want to doRead English, Japanese, Korean or Chinese on the phone in your language, laid over the original. Translation runs on this PC; the text on your screen is never sent to the internet.

  1. With the phone’s picture shown, click Translate on the toolbar (or CtrlL).
  2. The first time, it asks to download a translation model (about 50 MB, once): click Download.
  3. Click Translate again (or CtrlL) to close it.
Success looks like thisThe translated text appears where the original was. While translating, the picture pauses (“Translating · picture paused” at the top right; it moves below the toolbar while the toolbar is up): that’s normal and ends when you close the translation.
If it doesn’t work
  • It says the text is already in your language: nothing to translate.
  • Japanese or Korean isn’t recognised: Windows needs that language’s “Optical character recognition”; see here.
  • Only a small part: right-click menu → Translate → Translate an area (CtrlShiftL) and drag a box.

Magnifier: text too small

Click Zoom on the toolbar once for 2×, again for 4×, again to go back; Ctrl + wheel zooms where the mouse is. Right-click menu → Magnifier also has Freeze picture (CtrlP).

Updates

When there is a new version, a window says so: click Update now. Zizai Cast closes, installs and opens again by itself. Not now? Click Remind me later. You can also use right-click menu → Help → Check for updates.

8Remote control: control this PC from a phone

See this PC’s screen in the phone’s browser, hear its sound and use it with your fingers. No app on the phone (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Pick your situation first:

SituationUseSomeone at the PC?

Turn on remote control

Switch to the Remote control tab at the top of the main window and turn on the switch at the top. Remote control is off every time Zizai Cast starts (unless Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts is ticked in More settings). While a phone is connected, a “Remote control in progress” bar stays at the top of the PC’s screen; End or CtrlD ends it any time.

Once the fixed address is on, the top block is This PC’s address (works from anywhere): a small label over the address in big type, the QR code beside it, and the buttons Copy address and Add a temporary account. Temporary accounts have their own card under it; At home and Tailscale are one row each. Things you rarely need are in More settings: Change the fixed address password…, Fixed address (works from anywhere) (untick it to turn the fixed address off), Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts, and Sound (Send the PC's sound to the phone, Mute the PC while the phone plays sound, Sound from).

Situation 1: at home (same Wi-Fi)

What you want to doSit on the sofa and use the PC in the study from your phone.

  1. PC: Remote control tab → At home (same Wi-Fi) card → Show QR code (this turns the switch on if needed).
  2. With the phone on the same Wi-Fi, scan the QR code with the camera and tap the address.
  3. The PC asks “A phone wants to control this PC”: if it’s your phone, click Allow. Tick Remember this device, don't ask again so you won’t be asked again.
  4. On the phone, tap Tap for sound, or the speaker labelled Sound on at the bottom right, to hear the PC (iPhone requires that tap).
Success looks like thisThe PC’s screen is on the phone and “● Remote control in progress” is at the top of the PC’s screen.
If it doesn’t work
  • The phone keeps spinning on “Click ‘Allow’ on the PC”: click Allow on the PC (the window may be behind another one).
  • The page doesn’t open: the phone isn’t on the same Wi-Fi, or the router has “AP isolation” on. See can’t connect.

Situation 2: me, away from home (fixed address)

What you want to doReach your home PC over mobile data. The address never changes and you sign in with your own password. The PC needs Tailscale (free); the phone needs nothing.

Three things, about 15 minutes the first time① install Tailscale on the PC and sign in → ② let Tailscale allow Funnel (the public address) and HTTPS → ③ set a password in Zizai Cast and turn on the fixed address. After that you just open the address on the phone.

① Install Tailscale on the PC, sign up, sign in

  1. On the PC open tailscale.com/download/windows, download and install it (Windows 10 or later).
  2. A Tailscale icon appears next to the clock (click ^ if you don’t see it). Right-click it → Log in; a browser opens.
  3. Sign in with a Google, Microsoft, GitHub or Apple account (Tailscale has no passwords of its own and can’t sign up with just an email). The first time it asks what for: choose Personal use.
  4. Right-click the Tailscale icon again: your account and Connected mean you’re done.
Success looks like thisZizai Cast’s Away: Tailscale card no longer says “Tailscale isn’t on this PC yet” (if it still does, turn remote control off and on).

② Allow Funnel and HTTPS (a must for new accounts)

A new Tailscale account doesn’t allow a public address (Funnel) yet, and has no HTTPS certificates. It’s needed once; the easiest way is to let Zizai Cast walk you through it:

  1. Do ③ below first and click Turn on.
  2. The block says “Funnel isn’t turned on for your Tailscale account yet (needed once)” (or HTTPS certificates): click Open the setup page; Tailscale’s page opens in the browser; sign in with the same account and click Enable.
  3. Back in Zizai Cast, click Done, try again.

Or with a command (the same result):

  1. Right-click Start → Terminal (or search for “Command Prompt”).
  2. Paste this line and press Enter (copy it as it is; Zizai Cast puts its own settings in afterwards): 
    tailscale funnel --bg --https=8443 http://127.0.0.2:8091
  3. The first time it prints a link asking you to allow Funnel. Select and copy it, open it in the browser, sign in with the same account and approve (Enable). Tailscale turns on HTTPS certificates and Funnel for you.
  4. Back in the terminal, if it’s still waiting, run the same line once more; when there’s no error you can close the terminal.
Your PC’s name becomes publicWhen HTTPS is turned on, Tailscale warns that the machine name and your tailnet name are written to public certificate logs. If the PC is named after you, rename it first in Windows Settings → System → About → Rename this PC.

③ Set a password in Zizai Cast and turn on the fixed address

  1. In the Allow remote control block at the top of the Remote control tab, click Set a password (fixed address + temporary accounts); type it twice, at least 8 characters (longer is better, not used anywhere else). Click Save and turn on.
  2. The first time, wait a minute or two: the block says “Preparing the address, about 1–2 minutes” while Tailscale gets the HTTPS certificate; until then the phone can’t open the page. Zizai Cast checks it by itself and says “The fixed address is on” only when it works.
  3. The block becomes This PC’s address (works from anywhere) with https://your-pc-name.….ts.net:8443/ in big type and a QR code. Scan it with the phone, or click Copy address and send it to yourself.
  4. Open the address on the phone: there is only a password field; enter yours. After one sign-in the phone won’t ask again for 30 days. Add it to the home screen to open it with one tap. On the sign-in page 投投 and three pink friends watch you: they look away while you type the password, look sad and shake their heads at a wrong one and smile when you’re in (with Reduce motion on in the system they only change faces).
  5. To be reachable as soon as the PC starts: More settings → tick Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts.
Success looks like thisAfter the password the PC’s screen appears on the phone. Nobody needs to be at the PC.
If it doesn’t work
  • The block says Funnel or HTTPS certificates aren’t turned on for your Tailscale account yet: as in ②, click Open the setup page, enable it, then Done, try again.
  • “Tailscale isn’t signed in” or “didn’t answer”: open Tailscale on the PC, sign in, click Try again.
  • The block keeps saying “Preparing the address”: give it a little longer; after 5 minutes it says the address still didn’t answer: click Try again, or Use a temporary address for now (it changes).
  • It says “The fixed address is on” but the phone still can’t open it: see here.
  • 5 wrong passwords lock it for 15 minutes; wait and try again. Forgot it: only the PC can reset it, with More settings → Change the fixed address password….

Tailscale steps checked against (Oct 2026): tailscale.com/kb/1223 (Funnel: needs MagicDNS, HTTPS and the funnel attribute; running tailscale funnel walks you through enabling it; ports 443, 8443, 10000 only), /kb/1311 (the command, --bg, --https, off), /docs/how-to/set-up-https-certificates (Enable HTTPS on the DNS page, Let’s Encrypt certificates, names are public).

Another way: Tailscale on the phone too

With the Tailscale app on the phone, signed in to the same account, it works like at home: Away: Tailscale card → Show QR code, scan it once at home and add it to the home screen; when you’re out, keep Tailscale on in the phone. No password and no Funnel needed.

Add the page to the home screen

  • iPhone: open the page in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open the page in Chrome → ⋮ at the top right → Add to Home screen.

Situation 3: lend it to a friend

What you want to doA friend somewhere else should see or use your PC for a while (to help you with something), and lose access afterwards. Don’t give anyone your own fixed address password. Both ways work alike: the first time your friend connects after signing in, someone at the PC clicks “Allow” (by default it can’t be remembered; for a friend you lend to often, see Ask before someone connects). After that, the same phone with the same account (or the same temporary address) is not asked again when it changes the quality, switches screens, reconnects after a drop or reloads the page; after Deny, End, or once the account expires or is deleted, Allow is needed again.

Way A: a temporary account (recommended; the fixed address must be on)

  1. PC: the This PC’s address (works from anywhere) block at the top of the Remote control tab → Add a temporary account.
  2. The Account name is filled in for you (for example guest-4821; you can change it). Pick Valid for: One use, 1 hour, 24 hours or 7 days; Allowed to: Control or View only; Can transfer files (phone and PC, both ways) is ticked already; untick it if they shouldn’t send files (with View only the box is greyed: no files in View only). Click Create.
  3. Click Copy all and paste it to your friend. The address already carries the account (#u=guest-4821); the password is shown only now (lost it? Reset password).
  4. Your friend opens the address: the account is filled in, they only type the password. Your PC asks “A phone wants to control this PC”; click Allow once you’re sure it’s your friend. While the account is valid, the same phone gets back in without asking again.
  5. When it expires or its one use is over, the account is deleted by itself; or click Delete in the temporary accounts card below (a connected phone is cut off at once). At most 10.

Way B: a temporary address (no fixed address needed)

  1. PC: without a fixed address, click Create a temporary address in the Allow remote control block; with one, More settings → Temporary address (no app on the phone; works on mobile data). After 10–20 seconds a QR code and a 6-digit Passcode appear (also on the Temporary tab of the small window, whose tabs are Same Wi-Fi, Fixed address, Tailscale, Temporary, in the same order as the main page).
  2. Click Copy address and send the address to your friend; tell them the passcode separately. The address works once, within 10 minutes. The first time you create one, Zizai Cast first downloads a small component (about 52 MB, once only; needs the internet; the progress is shown).
  3. Your friend opens it and enters the passcode; your PC asks “A phone wants to control this PC” and you click “Allow” at the PC.
  4. When you’re done, click Stop the temporary address, or just turn remote control off.
Success looks like thisYour friend’s phone shows your PC, “● Remote control in progress” is at the top of your screen, and you can click End or press CtrlD any time.
If it doesn’t work
  • Your friend sees “This address is no longer valid”: an address works once, for 10 minutes; stop it and get a new one.
  • Your friend keeps waiting on “Click ‘Allow’ on the PC”: you must click Allow at the PC (temporary addresses and temporary accounts alike, once for every new sign-in).
  • Your friend’s Files button is grey: a tap says why. The temporary account was made without Can transfer files (make a new one with it ticked), or the session is in View only.
  • “The temporary address can't be reached from outside yet”: stop it and get a new one.
AfterwardsWhen your friend is done, click End and stop the temporary address. They can see your whole screen: close private windows first. To let them only watch, turn on View only in the bar at the top of the PC’s screen.

Using the phone: Direct or Trackpad

The phone page has six icons at the bottom right, each with a word under it: Sound on (then Sound), Zoom, Files (grey when files are off; a tap says why), Keyboard, Stats (latency, frames per second), Settings. The first time, a card says which mode is on, with a one-tap switch. In Settings → Control pick (the small line under it says what each does):

ControlHow it worksGood for
Direct (default)Your finger clicks where it touches; hold half a second = right-click; hold and drag = dragBig buttons, menus, beginners
TrackpadLike a laptop trackpad: slide to move the pointer, tap = left click, two-finger tap = right-click, two-finger slide = scrollSmall targets, selecting text

In Trackpad mode a big arrow (pink ring) shows where the PC pointer is, and a tap clicks there; if you keep tapping far from the arrow, the page offers to switch to Direct. Pinch to zoom the picture; a three-finger tap opens or closes the keyboard. The keyboard panel has Ctrl, Alt, Win, arrows, copy, paste and the clipboard.

Three rows in the phone page’s Settings are about the picture: Picture (Smooth / Standard / Sharp), Frame rate (30 / 60 / 120, 60 by default; if the PC’s screen runs at 60 Hz it sends at most 60 and the row says so) and Match the phone’s resolution (the PC sizes the picture to this phone’s screen: smoother, less data; on by default on phones and tablets, off in a computer’s browser). They all change inside the same connection: no black flash, no Allow again.

From another computer’s browser

Open the same address in a browser on another computer (Chrome, Edge …) and use its mouse and keyboard: left, right and middle buttons, back / forward buttons, the wheel and dragging all go through, and a click lands where you point. You type with that computer’s own input method (Chinese, Japanese, Korean too); shortcuts (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, F5 …) go to the remote PC, not to the browser.

  1. Move the mouse to the very top and the bar appears: Full screen, Actual size / Fit window, a button per screen (with two or more), Game mode, Alt+Tab, Win, Ctrl+Alt+Del, Ctrl+W, Task Manager, Clipboard, sound, Files, Stats, Settings.
  2. In Full screen, Alt+Tab, Win and the like go to the remote PC as well; hold Esc to leave full screen. If the browser won’t let the page take those keys, a card says which keys don’t get through: use the bar’s buttons, or Try again.
  3. Ctrl+V on this computer pastes its clipboard text on the remote PC; text copied on the remote PC comes to this computer’s clipboard.
  4. For games or 3D programs, click Game mode: the mouse is locked to the picture. Esc or Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q leaves; Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M next screen, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+S stats on / off (these three never reach the remote PC).
If it doesn’t work
  • A click does nothing and the page says “This window runs as administrator”: that program was started as administrator, so remote clicks can’t reach it. Open it normally, or run Zizai Cast as administrator.
  • Ctrl+Alt+Del doesn’t go through: it is Windows’ secure key and no program can send it. For Task Manager use Task Manager on the bar.
  • Files on the bar is grey: hover over it to see why (View only, or a temporary account without files).

Controlling Windows from a Mac

Open the same address in Safari or Chrome on a Mac (or an iPad with a keyboard) and use the shortcuts the Mac way:

  • ⌘ + a letter goes to Windows as Ctrl + that letter: ⌘C copy, ⌘V paste, ⌘X cut, ⌘Z undo, ⌘A select all, ⌘S save, ⌘F find, ⌘T, ⌘W …
  • ⌘ pressed alone is the Windows key (Start); ⌘ + an arrow is Win + that arrow; Option is Alt (Option+F4 closes a window).
  • The phone page’s Settings has Mac: Cmd as Ctrl, switched on by itself on a Mac / iPad; switched off, ⌘ is always the Windows key.
  • ⌘Q, ⌘W and ⌘Tab are taken by the Mac or the browser first; to send them to Windows use Full screen or the bar’s buttons at the top (for example Ctrl+W).

Ask before someone connects

PC: the Remote control tab → More settings → Ask before someone connects. Your account (fixed address + your password), guest accounts and the temporary address each get one choice:

ChoiceWhen someone connects from outside
Ask every timeEvery new sign-in needs Allow on the PC (the default for guest accounts and the temporary address)
Remember this deviceThe Allow dialog gets a “Remember” box; once ticked, the same device comes straight in next time
Password onlyThe right password lets it straight in, no Allow (for the temporary address: “Passcode only”)
  • Memories are kept per place (home, the fixed address, each guest account, the temporary address) and never shared between them.
  • Deny or End, a guest account deleted or expired, a guest password reset or a new main password forget the matching devices; a device that was ended always needs Allow once more.
  • More settings → Remembered phones: remove them one by one or forget them all; each row says where it was remembered.
  • Home (the same Wi-Fi) and Tailscale stay as they were: Allow every time (you can tick Remember).
  • Every entry in the Connection log says how the device got in, for example “Ask every time · you clicked Allow” or “Password only · right password, no Allow needed”.

Safety

  • The fixed address is public and only your password keeps others out: use a long one you don’t use anywhere else.
  • While connected, “● Remote control in progress” stays at the top of the PC’s screen; CtrlD ends it from any program.
  • After 30 minutes without a touch the phone is disconnected (change it in More settings).
  • More settings → Lock the PC after remote control ends: locks the PC when a session ends. Off by default (from outside and at home); turn it on if you want it.
  • No longer need outside access: in More settings, untick Turn the fixed address on when the PC starts and Fixed address (works from anywhere) (unticking it turns the fixed address off).

What it can’t do

  • Windows run as administrator, UAC prompts and the lock screen can’t be used from the phone (a Windows rule); when you click one, the phone page tells you why.
  • A PC that is off, asleep or at the sign-in screen can’t be reached. One phone at a time.
MoreSound, troubleshooting

9Sound volume

What you want to doFix sound that is too quiet, too loud or missing. First, which one is it?

Phone mirrored to the PC (sound from the PC)

  1. iPhone: just press the iPhone’s volume buttons; the PC’s volume follows and Zizai Cast remembers it.
  2. PC: right-click menu → Volume (e.g. “Volume: 50%”), or the speaker icon at the bottom right of the screen.
  3. Still silent: right-click the speaker icon → “Volume mixer” and check Zizai Cast isn’t muted.
Success looks like thisThe current volume shows briefly on the picture while you change it.

Remote control (the PC’s sound on the phone)

  1. The phone page starts silent (an iPhone rule): tap Tap for sound, or the speaker labelled Sound on at the bottom right.
  2. The PC sets a good level by itself, so Phone volume at its default 100% is usually enough. Still too quiet: Settings at the bottom right → Phone volume, slide right (0–400%). 100% is the PC’s own level; above 100% is “loudness boost”: louder the further right, quiet parts raised the most, never clipping.
  3. Turn up the phone’s own volume too, and switch off the iPhone’s silent switch.
  4. PC: Remote control tab → More settings → Sound: Send the PC's sound to the phone must be ticked. To keep the PC quiet, tick Mute the PC while the phone plays sound there, or turn on Mute the PC’s speakers in the phone page’s Settings (the same setting, kept on the PC; temporary accounts and “View only” can’t change it).
If it doesn’t work
  • “The PC isn’t playing any sound right now”: the PC really is silent.
  • Windows is muted on the PC: unmute it; to keep the PC quiet use “Mute the PC while the phone plays sound” (“Mute the PC’s speakers” on the phone page).
  • More: no sound in remote control, no sound while mirroring.

10Troubleshooting

Click a question to open the answer.

Phone mirroring

“Zizai Cast” isn’t in the phone’s list
  • Check Zizai Cast is running (icon at the bottom right).
  • Phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi, not a guest network, phone not on mobile data.
  • Turn off VPNs on both; check the firewall allows it (see Firewall).
  • School, office and hotel networks often block it: use a home network or a hotspot from the PC.
  • Close other receivers such as AirServer or LonelyScreen.
  • Turn the phone’s Wi-Fi off and on, or reopen Zizai Cast.
Picture but no sound while mirroring
  • Turn up the iPhone’s volume buttons and switch off its silent switch.
  • Check the PC’s speaker icon and the “Volume mixer” don’t mute Zizai Cast.
  • Changed headphones or speakers: reconnect once.
  • Some video apps block sound or picture while mirroring for copyright reasons; that isn’t Zizai Cast.
The picture lags or stutters
  • Use 5 GHz Wi-Fi on both, close to the router; a cable for the PC helps.
  • Right-click menu → Settings → Picture quality → Standard (smoothest).
  • Pause big downloads.
  • Remote control stutters: the phone page’s Settings → Picture → Smooth, turn on Match the phone’s resolution, Frame rate 60.
The picture is blurry
  • Right-click menu → Settings → Picture quality → High or Highest; if they are grey, install “HEVC Video Extensions” from the Microsoft Store.
  • After a change, click Reconnect now to apply next to the hint.
Another phone took over in the middle

Right-click menu → Settings → When a second phone connects → Keep the current one; you can also turn on Ask new phones for a PIN.

Firewall: I clicked “Cancel” the first time
  1. Start → search for “Allow an app through Windows Firewall”.
  2. Click Change settings, find “自在投影” (Zizai Cast) and tick Private.
  3. Not in the list: Allow another app… and pick “自在投影.exe” in the install folder.
It stops working with a VPN on

Disconnect the VPN on the PC and the phone while mirroring. Some VPNs have an “Allow LAN access” option that also works.

Android casting (Miracast) doesn’t connect
  • The PC’s Wi-Fi must be on and its mobile hotspot off.
  • Check support: right-click Start → Terminal, type netsh wlan show drivers and look for “Wireless Display Supported”: both must be Yes.
  • No, or a Google Pixel: use Android control.
Android control drops or doesn’t reconnect next time
  • Android turns Wireless debugging off when the phone restarts (often after a Wi-Fi change too), and Zizai Cast says once “Can’t find the phone you paired before…”: turn it back on in Developer options, no new pairing needed.
  • Battery saving may cut Wi-Fi when the screen is off: turn on Keep the phone awake while connected.
  • A phone you disconnected with CtrlD doesn’t come back by itself: use “Connect Android (scan QR)” or reopen Zizai Cast.
  • Still no luck: remove this PC under “Wireless debugging → Paired devices” on the phone and pair again.
Where is “Disconnect”?

Move the mouse to the top of the picture: the red button at the right end of the toolbar. Or CtrlD, or right-click the icon at the bottom right → Disconnect. On an Android picture right-click is “Back”; for the menu use Shift + right-click.

Translate does nothing, or Japanese / Korean isn’t translated
  • The first time a model is downloaded: click Download when asked (about 50 MB; Japanese or Korean into Chinese about 100 MB).
  • Japanese or Korean not recognised: click Open language settings in the hint, or Windows Settings → Time & language → Language & region → Add a language → Japanese (Korean) → tick only “Optical character recognition” → Install.

Remote control

The fixed address won’t start (new Tailscale account)
  • A new account must allow Funnel and HTTPS first: click Open the setup page in the block, or do ② Allow Funnel and HTTPS once.
  • Or in the Tailscale admin console (login.tailscale.com/admin), DNS page: turn on MagicDNS and click Enable HTTPS under HTTPS Certificates, then add the funnel permission in Access controls. The command is easier.
  • Tailscale must be running and signed in on the PC.
The fixed address is new and the phone shows ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED

The first time, Tailscale has to get an HTTPS certificate; until then the page can’t open. Zizai Cast checks it by itself: while the block says “Preparing the address, about 1–2 minutes”, wait; it says “The fixed address is on” only once it works. If it says so and the phone still can’t open it, the phone’s DNS may not know the new name yet (Tailscale says up to 10 minutes): wait and reload. Still not: on the PC, untick Fixed address (works from anywhere) in More settings and tick it again.

The phone can’t reach the PC
  • The QR code at home doesn’t open: the phone isn’t on the same Wi-Fi (guest networks and mobile data don’t work), or the router has AP isolation on.
  • The fixed address doesn’t open: the PC must be on, signed in, with Tailscale running; the first time, see the question above.
  • The page opens but says “The picture connection is blocked”: Windows Firewall blocked it (click Allow access the first time you turn remote control on), AP isolation, or another VPN.
  • “This address is no longer valid”: scan the QR code on the PC again (and re-add the home screen icon).
No sound or too quiet in remote control
  • Tap Tap for sound (or the speaker labelled Sound on); still quiet: phone page Settings → Phone volume, slide right (100% by default; above 100% is loudness boost, up to 400%).
  • The iPhone’s silent switch and the phone’s volume.
  • Don’t mute Windows on the PC; More settings → Sound → Send the PC's sound to the phone must be ticked.
I forgot the fixed address password

Only the PC can reset it: Remote control tab → More settings → Change the fixed address password…. Phones then sign in with the new one.

My PC got locked

If it’s turned on (it’s off by default), the PC locks when a remote session ends. Unlock it with your Windows password or PIN. Don’t want it: More settings → untick Lock the PC after remote control ends.

Is it still running after I close the window?

Yes, it waits in the background at the bottom right. To quit completely: right-click the icon → Quit.
